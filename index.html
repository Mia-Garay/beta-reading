Manuscript fit

Beta reading is for a polished manuscript.

Your manuscript does not need professional editing first, but it should have gone through several rounds of self-editing and be as close to publishable as you can make it on your own.

Best genre fit

Primary: Adult and YA fantasy, science fiction, mystery, and thriller.

Selectively open to: Romance, historical fiction, nonfiction, and literary fiction.

Not accepted

Westerns, horror, memoirs, and children’s books.

I may also decline a manuscript when I believe it needs more self-editing or when I am not the right reader for what the author needs.

Frequently asked questions

Before you request a beta read.

Is beta reading the same as editing?

No. Beta reading focuses on your experience as a reader: what is clear, confusing, engaging, slow, believable, or emotionally effective. I may point out wording or grammar when it interrupts the reading experience, but I do not edit or rewrite your manuscript.

Will you tell me whether my book is ready to publish?

I can tell you how the manuscript worked for me as a reader and where I believe it may need more attention. The final decision about whether it is ready to publish remains yours.

Do you read the entire manuscript?

Yes. Once I accept a paid beta read, I read the entire manuscript unless an unexpected issue makes it necessary to stop. If that happens, I will contact you and explain the situation.

Will you give me progress updates?

I do not send automatic progress updates, but you are welcome to ask how the read is going. I deliver the full feedback together so my reading experience is not influenced by an ongoing discussion about the manuscript.

Can I send you my own questionnaire?

Yes. When you provide a beta-reader questionnaire, I complete it in addition to the written report.

Can you sign an NDA?

Yes. I am willing to review and sign a reasonable author-provided NDA or beta-reader agreement.

Do you use AI to read manuscripts?

No. I personally read every word. Your manuscript is never uploaded to AI, and AI is never used to read, analyze, or evaluate your story.
