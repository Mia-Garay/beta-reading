Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information I collect, why I collect it, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Who operates this site: Mia Garay is the professional name used by Maria Cordero, a Texas sole proprietor. Questions about this policy may be sent to miagaray@miagaray.com .

1. Information I collect

I may collect information that you choose to provide when you visit the website, submit a beta-reading request, schedule a call, make a payment, join an email list, send an email, or work with me. This may include:

  • Your name, email address, and other contact information.
  • Information about you, your manuscript, your publishing plans, your requested services, your deadline, and your feedback needs.
  • Payment and transaction information. Payment card details are processed by Stripe and are not stored directly by me.
  • Messages, questionnaires, agreements, invoices, scheduling details, and other records connected to your request or service.
  • Your manuscript and related materials when you become a client.

The website host and other service providers may also receive technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, and basic usage or security data.

2. How I use information

I use information to:

  • Review and respond to requests.
  • Decide whether a project is a good fit.
  • Provide beta-reading services and follow-up support.
  • Process payments, keep business records, prevent fraud, and comply with legal or tax obligations.
  • Send service-related messages and, when you have subscribed or otherwise permitted it, marketing emails.
  • Maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the website and business systems.
  • Protect my rights, clients, confidential materials, and the integrity of the service.

3. Manuscripts and confidential materials

I treat manuscripts and related client materials as confidential. I personally read each manuscript. Manuscript text is not uploaded to artificial intelligence tools for reading, analysis, summarization, or evaluation.

Manuscripts are normally shared through Google Docs with commenter access. You keep ownership of your work. I use manuscript content only to provide the requested service, communicate with you, maintain necessary business records, or comply with law.

4. Service providers

I use third-party services to operate my business. Depending on how you interact with me, these may include:

  • GitHub Pages: Hosting my beta-reading services website and custom beta-reading intake form.
  • Google services: Email, documents, and file storage. Google Apps Script processes submissions from my custom intake form, and Google Sheets stores the submitted request information.
  • Kit: Email marketing, subscriber management, and related business services.
  • Stripe: Payment processing and transaction records.
  • Cal.com: Scheduling consultations and other appointments.

These providers process information under their own privacy policies and terms. I share only the information reasonably needed for them to provide their services.

5. Email communications

Service emails are used to respond to requests and provide purchased services. Marketing emails are sent only when permitted.

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in the message. Unsubscribing from marketing does not prevent necessary service, payment, legal, or transaction-related messages.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not currently use advertising cookies or behavioral advertising, and I have not intentionally installed analytics tracking on the beta-reading services website.

The website includes a cookie preference notice. When you accept or reject optional cookies, your selection is saved using your browser's local storage. This allows the website to remember your preference when you return.

Local storage is a browser feature that can save information on your device. It is not the same as an advertising cookie. The current preference notice records your selection but does not automatically block third-party tracking technologies.

You can review or change your saved preference by selecting Cookie Settings in the footer of the main beta-reading services page. You can also clear your browser's stored website data.

GitHub Pages, Google Fonts, and other third-party services used to operate the website may receive technical information, including your IP address, when providing their services.

If I introduce nonessential analytics, advertising, or other tracking technologies in the future, I will review and update this policy and the website's consent controls as appropriate.

7. How long information is kept

I keep information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to complete services, preserve appropriate business and tax records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with law.

Manuscripts are generally shared through Google Docs, where the author retains ownership and control of the original document. Authors may revoke my access after the beta read is complete.

Any manuscript copies or working files that I maintain are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to complete the service or fulfill legitimate business or legal obligations.

I may retain limited business records, correspondence, and my own feedback notes when reasonably necessary for recordkeeping, follow-up, or resolving disputes.

8. How information may be disclosed

I do not sell personal information. I may disclose information:

  • To service providers that help operate the business.
  • When you direct or authorize me to do so.
  • To comply with law, court orders, valid legal requests, tax obligations, or professional advice.
  • To protect rights, safety, confidential information, or the security of the website and business.
  • As part of a business transfer, reorganization, or similar transaction, subject to appropriate confidentiality protections.

9. Your privacy choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information that I hold about you, subject to legal, contractual, security, and recordkeeping exceptions.

You may also ask questions about how your information is used.

You can review or change your optional-cookie preference through Cookie Settings on the main beta-reading services page . You can also clear stored website data through your browser settings.

Send privacy requests to miagaray@miagaray.com . I may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

10. Information about children

The website and beta-reading services are intended for adults. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through this website.

A parent or legal guardian who believes a child has provided information may contact me to request its removal.

11. International visitors

The business is based in Texas, United States. Information may be processed and stored in the United States and in other locations used by service providers.

Visitors outside the United States may have additional rights under local law.

12. Security

I use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect personal information and confidential manuscripts.

No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

13. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy as the website, services, providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top will show when the policy was last revised.
