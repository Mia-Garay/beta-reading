Refund Policy

This policy explains when a beta-reading payment may be refunded, partially refunded, credited, or retained.

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Beta reading reserves time on my schedule and involves work that cannot be returned once performed. Refund eligibility depends on when cancellation is requested, whether work has begun, and the circumstances surrounding the request.

1. Twenty-four-hour cancellation window

You may cancel your beta-reading purchase within 24 hours after payment and receive a full refund.

To request cancellation, email miagaray@miagaray.com within 24 hours of your payment.

Requests received during this cancellation window qualify for a full refund of the amount paid, without deductions for payment-processing fees.

This cancellation window applies regardless of how far in advance your reading period has been scheduled.

2. Before reading begins

After the initial 24-hour cancellation window, you may still request cancellation before your agreed reading start date.

If no substantive project work has begun, I will generally issue a full refund unless a specific nonrefundable charge was clearly disclosed and agreed to before payment.

When project-specific preparation or other substantive work has already been completed, a full or partial refund may be appropriate, depending on the circumstances.

I will consider the work actually performed, any previously disclosed and agreed charges, and the timing of the cancellation.

Simply reserving a future reading date does not automatically mean the entire payment becomes nonrefundable.

If I cancel the project before substantive work begins for a reason unrelated to your actions, I will issue a full refund.

Any proposed partial refund will be explained before it is processed.

3. After reading begins

Once substantive reading has begun, refund requests are considered individually. The outcome depends on the reason for cancellation and the amount of work already completed.

If you cancel after reading has begun, I may retain payment for work already completed and any applicable charges previously disclosed and agreed upon.

If I am unable to complete the project because of my own circumstances, you may choose to accept a revised delivery date or request cancellation.

If cancellation is requested because I cannot complete the agreed service, I will provide an appropriate refund for the portion of work not completed or delivered.

If little or no substantive work has been completed, a full refund may be appropriate.

Completed and delivered beta-reading services are not refundable solely because you disagree with the feedback, decide not to use it, revise your manuscript, change your publishing plans, or expected a different reader response.

When a partial refund is appropriate, I will explain how the amount was determined.

4. Failure to provide manuscript access

Before payment, we will agree upon a reading start date and delivery date based on your manuscript, purchased package, and my availability.

You must provide complete and usable Google Docs commenter access before our agreed reading start date, unless another arrangement has been confirmed in writing.

Payment or manuscript access received ahead of the agreed start date does not cause reading to begin earlier.

If manuscript access is not available when the reading period is scheduled to begin, I may need to postpone the project.

We will discuss a revised reading start date and delivery date based on my availability.

The original delivery date cannot be guaranteed when the required manuscript access has not been provided on time.

If the project cannot proceed because the manuscript or required access is not provided, cancellation and refund eligibility will be determined under this policy.

Any refund decision will consider work already completed, previously disclosed and agreed charges, and the circumstances of the delay.

Failure to provide manuscript access does not automatically result in forfeiture of the entire payment.

5. Manuscript or conduct problems

I may stop the service if the manuscript materially differs from what was disclosed during the request and acceptance process.

This may include substantial differences in word count, genre, manuscript condition, content warnings, or the agreed scope of work.

I may also stop reading if the manuscript contains undisclosed extreme material, appears plagiarized or unlawful, or creates a serious safety or ethical concern.

Service may also be discontinued if communications become threatening, abusive, harassing, discriminatory, fraudulent, or otherwise unsafe.

If the project stops because of inaccurate disclosures, author conduct, removal of access, or another author-caused issue, a refund is not guaranteed.

Any refund will take into account work already completed and any applicable, previously agreed charges.

Nothing in this section overrides the 24-hour cancellation guarantee or rights that cannot legally be waived.

6. Delays and rescheduling

My beta-reading turnaround periods are measured in calendar days, including weekends and holidays.

Your agreed reading start date counts as Day 1. The delivery date is based on the purchased package and any additional time agreed upon in writing.

Payment or manuscript access received early does not begin the reading period before the agreed start date.

If the manuscript is delayed, access becomes unavailable, necessary information is missing, or you request a change to the agreed scope, we may need to establish new dates.

A delay caused by the author does not automatically create a right to a refund or require me to maintain the original delivery date.

Any revised reading start date and delivery date must be confirmed based on my availability.

If I need a material extension because of illness, an emergency, or another circumstance affecting my ability to complete the service, I will communicate with you as soon as reasonably possible.

You may accept the revised schedule or request cancellation.

When cancellation results from my inability to complete the agreed service, the refund will reflect the amount of work completed and the portion remaining undelivered.

If the project has not begun and I cannot honor the agreed schedule or provide a mutually acceptable alternative, I will issue a full refund.

7. Payment-processing fees

Refunds are returned to the original payment method whenever possible.

When a full refund is approved, you will receive the full amount paid, without deductions for payment-processing fees.

Stripe generally does not return its original payment-processing fees to me when a transaction is refunded.

These processing fees are my responsibility and will not be deducted from an approved full refund.

If a partial refund is approved, I will confirm the refund amount and explain the calculation. The approved refund amount will not be reduced by additional payment-processing deductions.

The time required for refunded funds to appear in your account depends on Stripe, your payment method, and your financial institution.

8. Chargebacks

If you have a concern about a payment, cancellation, or refund, I encourage you to contact me first so I can review the situation and attempt to resolve it.

Filing a payment dispute or chargeback does not change the documentation of the services provided or the terms of the original agreement.

If a chargeback occurs, I may provide Stripe or the relevant financial institution with appropriate supporting documentation.

This may include the original request, invoice, payment record, written communications, manuscript access records, and evidence of work completed or delivered.

Nothing in this policy prevents you from exercising any dispute rights available through your payment provider or applicable law.

9. How to request a cancellation or refund

To request cancellation or a refund, email miagaray@miagaray.com .

Please include the following information when available:

  • Your name and the email address used for payment.
  • The beta-reading package or invoice involved.
  • Whether manuscript access has been provided and whether reading has begun.
  • The reason for your request, if you wish to provide one.

A reason for cancellation is not required for a refund requested within the 24-hour cancellation window.

I will review the request and respond with the applicable refund decision, any relevant calculation, and the expected processing timeline.

Requests made within the 24-hour cancellation window will be evaluated according to the time the cancellation email was received.

10. Legal rights

This Refund Policy does not remove, restrict, or waive any rights or protections that cannot legally be waived under applicable law.

This policy should be read alongside my Beta Reading Terms and Terms of Service .

Where a project-specific written agreement provides additional or different terms, those terms apply to the extent permitted by law and provided they do not remove nonwaivable legal protections.
