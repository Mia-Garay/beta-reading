Beta Reading Terms

These terms explain what my beta-reading services include, what they do not include, and the responsibilities of both the author and the beta reader.

Effective date: November 11, 2026

These terms apply to paid beta-reading services provided by Maria Cordero under the professional name Mia Garay. A project-specific email, invoice, form, or written agreement may establish additional details, including word count, price, scope, the agreed reading start date, and the delivery date.

1. Nature of beta reading

Beta reading provides one reader's honest experience of a manuscript. My feedback reflects how the story worked for me as a reader.

Feedback may address clarity, engagement, pacing, emotional impact, characterization, plot logic, inconsistencies, worldbuilding, reader expectations, and other areas requested by the author.

Beta reading is not developmental editing, line editing, copyediting, proofreading, fact-checking, sensitivity reading, legal review, or a guarantee that every error or issue will be identified.

I provide reader reactions and observations. I do not rewrite the manuscript or take responsibility for deciding how the author should revise the story.

2. Request and acceptance

You must provide accurate information about your manuscript, including genre, word count, stage of completion, requested feedback, scheduling needs, series context, and relevant content warnings.

I may ask additional questions or offer a free 15-minute consultation before deciding whether to accept your project.

I may decline a project when it falls outside my genre preferences, availability, manuscript readiness requirements, or the type of feedback I provide.

Submitting a request or participating in a consultation does not automatically create a client relationship or reserve a place on my schedule.

Before payment, we will confirm the accepted manuscript, package, price, reading start date, delivery date, and any additional arrangements.

A service agreement begins once I accept the project, we confirm the relevant terms, and payment is completed.

3. Manuscript condition

The manuscript must be complete for the portion purchased and should have gone through several rounds of self-editing.

Professional editing is not required before beta reading. However, the manuscript should be as close to publishable as you can reasonably make it through your own revisions.

I do not accept unrevised first drafts. The manuscript submitted for beta reading should be the version you intend for me to read.

If the submitted manuscript materially differs from the description, word count, genre, version, or scope that was accepted, I may pause the service and propose a revised price or delivery schedule, or cancel the project under the Refund Policy .

4. Packages, word count, and scope

My standard beta-reading packages are:

  • First Chapter: $20 for up to 5,000 words, with a turnaround of 3 calendar days. Includes inline reader reactions, a written beta report of approximately one page, completion of an author questionnaire when provided, and email follow-up.
  • Partial Manuscript: $85 for up to 40,000 words, with a turnaround of 12 calendar days. Includes inline reader reactions, a written beta report of approximately two pages, completion of an author questionnaire when provided, and email follow-up.
  • Full Manuscript: $165 for up to 90,000 words, with a turnaround of 21 calendar days. Includes inline reader reactions, a written beta report of approximately two to three pages, completion of an author questionnaire when provided, email follow-up, and one 30-minute follow-up call.

For manuscripts exceeding 90,000 words, each additional increment of up to 10,000 words adds $20 and 3 calendar days unless another arrangement is confirmed in writing.

All turnaround periods are measured in calendar days, including weekends and holidays. The actual reading start date and delivery date are confirmed before payment.

Report lengths are approximate. Inline comments are based on meaningful reader reactions and are not promised at a fixed number or frequency.

If a different package, rate, scope, or turnaround is agreed upon in writing, those project-specific details control.

5. Payment and manuscript access

Payment is due in full before your reading period is reserved and before reading begins.

Once we have agreed upon your reading start date and delivery date, payment confirms your reservation for that reading period.

After payment, you will provide your manuscript through Google Docs with commenter access, unless we agree to another method in writing.

Complete and usable manuscript access must be provided before the agreed reading start date.

Payment or manuscript access received ahead of the agreed start date does not cause reading to begin earlier.

You are responsible for maintaining your own backup of the manuscript. I am not responsible for loss caused by deletion, access removal, file corruption, changes made by others, or problems with a third-party platform outside my reasonable control.

6. Deadline and delays

Your reading start date and delivery date are agreed upon before the project begins. These dates are based on the accepted package, manuscript word count, and my availability.

The agreed reading start date counts as Day 1. The completed feedback is due on the final calendar day of the agreed turnaround period.

Weekends and holidays are included in the turnaround calculation. Business-day counting is not used.

Reading begins on the agreed start date, provided full payment has been completed and usable manuscript access is available.

If payment or manuscript access is delayed beyond the agreed start date, reading may need to be rescheduled.

In that situation, we will confirm a revised reading start date and delivery date based on my availability. The original delivery date cannot be guaranteed when the author has not met the agreed requirements.

Dates may also need to be adjusted because of circumstances outside reasonable control, including illness, emergencies, service outages, or inaccessible manuscript files.

If I encounter a circumstance that may affect the agreed delivery date, I will communicate with you as soon as reasonably possible. We will discuss the available options, including a revised date or cancellation under the Refund Policy.

7. Deliverables

By the agreed delivery date, I will provide the deliverables included in your purchased package. Depending on the package, these include:

  • Reader reactions and observations left as inline comments in your Google Doc.
  • A written beta report summarizing my overall reading experience.
  • Responses to your beta-reader questionnaire, when one was provided and included in the agreed scope.
  • Email follow-up to clarify delivered comments and feedback.

Every package includes reasonable email follow-up about the feedback delivered.

Full Manuscript Beta Read: The full-manuscript package also includes one 30-minute follow-up call at no additional charge.

The call is intended to discuss and clarify the feedback provided in the beta report and inline comments. It takes place after the completed feedback has been delivered and must be scheduled at a mutually agreed time.

The First Chapter and Partial Manuscript packages do not include a follow-up call as part of their standard package.

Follow-up support is intended to clarify feedback already provided. It does not include rereading a revised manuscript, evaluating newly written scenes, performing editing services, or preparing a new report unless separately agreed upon.

8. Reader independence and feedback

My feedback reflects my personal reading experience and professional judgment as a beta reader.

You may accept, reject, or adapt any observations I provide.

You remain solely responsible for revising the manuscript, making publishing decisions, confirming factual accuracy, complying with applicable laws, and determining the final content of your work.

I do not guarantee publication readiness, commercial success, acceptance by agents or publishers, positive reviews, book sales, rankings, or a particular response from future readers.

9. Author questionnaire

You may provide a reasonable beta-reader questionnaire to identify specific areas where you would like reader feedback.

The questionnaire should be provided before the agreed reading start date so I can consider your questions while reading.

Questions submitted after reading has begun may be declined or may require additional time or a separate agreement.

I will answer questions based on my actual experience of the manuscript. I cannot guarantee that every question will have a definitive answer or that my reactions will match the author's expectations.

10. Confidentiality and ownership

You retain all copyrights, ownership rights, and other intellectual-property rights in your manuscript.

I receive only the limited permission necessary to access, read, and comment on the manuscript for the purpose of providing the agreed beta-reading service.

I treat manuscripts and related materials as confidential, whether or not a separate confidentiality agreement has been signed.

I will not publish, distribute, reproduce for public use, or share your manuscript with unauthorized individuals.

I am willing to review and sign a reasonable author-provided nondisclosure agreement or beta-reader agreement before manuscript access is granted.

Any conflicting contractual terms must be resolved in writing before the project begins.

Manuscripts are normally shared through Google Docs. You retain control of the original document and may revoke my access after the service is complete.

Information about how manuscript materials and business records are handled is also available in my Privacy Policy .

11. Human-only reading and AI

I personally read every manuscript accepted for beta reading.

I do not upload manuscript text to artificial intelligence tools for reading, analysis, summarization, evaluation, rewriting, or generation.

The feedback I provide is based on my own reading experience, not an AI-generated evaluation of your manuscript.

I may use administrative tools, including AI-assisted tools, to organize, format, or clean up my own notes. Manuscript text is not supplied to those tools.

Ordinary business services may also be used for scheduling, payment processing, email communication, document access, and recordkeeping, consistent with my Privacy Policy.

12. Content, safety, and right to stop

You must provide accurate content warnings for material that could reasonably affect whether I accept your manuscript.

I may stop reading if a manuscript contains undisclosed extreme material, unlawful material, apparent plagiarism, or content that creates a serious safety or ethical concern.

I may also discontinue the service when communications become threatening, harassing, abusive, fraudulent, or otherwise unsafe.

If a paid project is stopped, any refund or other financial resolution will depend on the circumstances and the amount of work completed, as explained in the Refund Policy .

13. Communication and progress updates

Email is the primary method of communication throughout the beta-reading process.

You are responsible for providing a working email address and checking your messages, including spam or promotions folders.

Automatic progress updates are not included. However, you may request a reasonable status update while the project is underway.

Feedback is normally delivered together when the beta read is complete. This allows me to finish the reading experience without ongoing discussion influencing my reactions.

If a problem arises that may affect the agreed scope or deadline, I will communicate with you as soon as reasonably possible.

14. Revisions and additional work

The beta-reading service covers the manuscript version submitted for the agreed reading start date.

Replacing the manuscript, making substantial changes during the read, requesting a second read, or expanding the requested scope may require a new agreement, additional payment, or revised dates.

My original beta-reading feedback does not include reviewing the author's revisions or determining whether suggested changes were successful.

Any additional work must be discussed and agreed upon separately.

15. Refunds and cancellations

You may request cancellation within 24 hours after payment for a full refund, subject to the conditions described in the Refund Policy.

After the cancellation window, refund eligibility depends on the circumstances, including whether reading or other project work has begun and how much work has been completed.

If you are unable to provide the manuscript or required access before the agreed reading start date, we will discuss revised dates or cancellation.

Cancellations, project interruptions, inability to complete services, and partial or full refunds are governed by the separate Refund Policy , which forms part of these terms.

Nothing in these terms removes any rights that cannot legally be waived.
